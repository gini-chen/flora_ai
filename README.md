## Project Aims

FloraAI combines floral customization, AI visualization, and financial analysis to explore whether a small floral business can be financially sustainable.

### Financial Analysis
- Track flower, packaging, transportation, and labor costs.
- Compare selling prices with estimated total costs.
- Estimate break-even prices and potential profit per arrangement.
- Build a dashboard to compare pricing and cost scenarios.

### AI Bouquet Visualization Website
- Develop AI-generated bouquet previews based on customers’ flower, color, and style preferences.
- Test whether previews help customers communicate their design preferences.

### Future Machine Learning
- Collect structured data from additional bouquet tests.
- Explore models that predict material costs or preparation time.
- Compare predictions with simple estimates to evaluate whether machine learning improves accuracy.

## Current Focus
Complete the prototype, troubleshoot AI image generation, and collect reliable cost and preparation-time data before developing predictive models.
